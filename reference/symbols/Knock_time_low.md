# Knock_time_low

**Value in factory 4780656:** (timer ticks)

**Function:** No-signal duration timer for the ion-path watchdog; latches fault past the 10 s threshold. Listed in the Error_frame layout (book p104).

**Code path:** F_0005C34A.
