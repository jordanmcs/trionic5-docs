
# Temp_limit_high

Calibration scalar · ROM 0x042897

**Value in factory 4780656:**

Raw **217** (1 B) at ROM `0x042897`.

**Function:** Upper bound of the adaptation temperature window (see [Temp_limit_low](Temp_limit_low.md)). Byte 217 = −39 °C signed.

**Code path:** Ignition-adaptation fade window.
