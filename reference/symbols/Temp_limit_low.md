
# Temp_limit_low

Calibration scalar · ROM 0x042896

**Value in factory 4780656:**

Raw **216** (1 B) at ROM `0x042896`.

**Function:** Lower bound of the adaptation temperature window; with −40 °C as the boundary the window is never entered in practice (adaptation temperature fade effectively disabled). Byte 216 = −40 °C signed.

**Code path:** Ignition-adaptation fade window (also in knock research).
