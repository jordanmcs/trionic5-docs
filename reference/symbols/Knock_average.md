# Knock_average

**Value in factory 4780656:** 0.1° units

**Function:** Mean of the four cylinder retard offsets; compared against Knock_average_limit for sustained-knock escalation.

**Code path:** Computed in offset engine; compared in diag path (0x5A2A8).
