# Eft_dec

**Value in factory 4780656:**

After-start decay rate from [Eft_dec_fak](Eft_dec_fak.md) (−1 at load).

**Function:** While non-zero, drives per-tick subtraction from [Eft_fak](Eft_fak.md) — the enrichment walk-down.

**Code path:** Loaded 0x6F28A/0x6F298; gate 0x6F2DC.
