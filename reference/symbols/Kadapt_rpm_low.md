
# Kadapt_rpm_low

Calibration word (RAM 0x5AC4)  ·  ROM 0x4286E

**Value in factory 4780656:** 210 (rpm/10 = 2100 rpm)

**Function:** Lower rpm bound of the K-adapt learning window.

**Code path:** Gate in F_000610D2.
